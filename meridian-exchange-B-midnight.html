    For the owner

    Built to fill your counter and save you time.

    This section is only in the sales demo. Every feature below works on this page, so try them.

    Update rates in seconds

    Change a rate in one table and the calculator, the banknote and every reservation follow. No web designer, no waiting.

    Turn browsers into walk-ins

    Customers lock today's rate for 2 hours and get a ticket to show at the counter. You see who is coming and for how much.

    Faster pickups

    The customer sees exactly which bills they will get, large or small. Your team gets a ready order slip instead of doing the math at the counter.

    One template, every branch

    Each branch gets its own address, phone and pin on the globe. Adding a branch is a new entry, not a new website.

    What we connect at launch

    • Reservations sent to your phone and email
    • Your logo, colors, photos and real address
    • A staff login for the rate editor
    • Rates from your own source or one staff screen
    • Search-friendly pages on fast hosting
    • Your other branches, added from the same template
